# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, experience level, nationality, or background.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Welcoming feedback and different viewpoints
- Focusing on what is best for the community

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

**hello@innovayse.com**

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly.
